# TCG Binder 1.4.0

A proper card viewer.

## Added

- **Card detail modal**: clicking a card in a collection or deck now opens a viewer — large scan, set and rarity, market price, legality badges — instead of jumping straight to the note. The note is still one click away via "Open card note".
- **Flip through your cards**: previous/next buttons (and the keyboard arrow keys) walk the exact list on screen — collection filters, search and sort are respected, and decks follow the Pokémon / Trainer / Energy grouping. Neighbor scans are preloaded, so browsing feels instant.
- **Full card text on demand**: abilities, attacks with cost and damage, trainer rules, retreat cost, flavor text and illustrator are fetched from the card database when the viewer opens (in your configured card language) and cached while you browse. Manual cards and offline use degrade gracefully — the viewer still shows everything stored in the note.
