# TCG Binder 1.12.0

Your decks and your collection finally agree on who has what.

## Added

- **"Own all" on the deck view**: one click registers every card of a deck as owned. A modal groups the deck by card type (Supporters, Items, Fire Pokémon, ...) and you pick the destination of each group — an existing collection, a newly created one with the suggested name, or skip it. Quantities only top up to what the deck runs, so re-running never inflates your counts, and cards without a known type get their own row instead of being silently dropped.
- **Clear wishlist**: a command and a button on the wishlist view empty it in one confirmed step — for after that big order arrives.
- **Reserved copies, explained**: when "Reserve deck copies" is on, the missing list now tells you the math — "4 in collection · 3 reserved by other decks" — instead of showing a number that looks wrong.
- **"Assembled" toggle per deck**: a deck that is still just a list can opt out of reserving copies it does not physically contain. Untick it and the deck that actually holds the card shows complete, while the list keeps showing what it needs.
- **Missing-cards dot on the dashboard**: deck rows get a subtle dot when the collection can't cover the deck, with the exact count on hover — same math as the deck view.

## Fixed

- **Dates were stamped in UTC**: cards added in the evening (west of Greenwich) were dated tomorrow. Acquisition dates, price-updated stamps, portfolio snapshots and the "added in the last N days" filter now use your local date.
- **Views opened halfway down**: entering a collection or deck (or going back to the dashboard) inherited the previous screen's scroll position. Every screen now opens at the top.
