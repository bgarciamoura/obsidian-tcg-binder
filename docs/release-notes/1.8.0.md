# TCG Binder 1.8.0

Covers, a smarter wishlist, and everything manageable from the dashboard.

## Added

- **Cover images**: give every collection and deck a cover — pick any card from the list and its art becomes a banner filling the left half of the dashboard card, with a diagonal edge. Adjust the vertical crop with a live preview slider, or remove the cover, from the same menu.
- **Missing cards go to the wishlist**: importing a deck automatically wishlists whatever your collections can't cover, and every deck has an "Add to wishlist" button on its missing-cards section. Both are idempotent — the wishlist is topped up to what's needed, never inflated.
- **Mark wishlist cards as acquired**: bought it? One click moves the card from the wishlist into the collection you choose, keeping quantity, variant and condition.
- **Delete decks and collections in the plugin**: a hover action on the dashboard with a confirmation dialog. The note goes to the trash (your Obsidian setting decides where); card notes are never touched.
- **Back to top**: a floating button appears once you scroll deep into a long collection, deck or the dashboard.
- **New setting — "Reserve cards used in decks"**: for players who keep every deck assembled. When on, copies used by other decks count as unavailable when computing what a deck is missing. Off by default: any owned copy satisfies every deck.
