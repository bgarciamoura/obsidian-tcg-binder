# TCG Binder 1.7.0

Cleaner collections and your own card images.

## Added

- **Upload your own card image**: cards the database has no scan for (brand-new promos, hand-written manual cards) show an "Add an image" button in the card viewer. The image is stored inside your binder folder, embedded in the card note, and shows up everywhere — viewer, grids, tables and thumbnails. "Fetch missing images" leaves these cards alone, and a later official scan never overwrites your upload.

## Fixed

- **Zeroing a card now removes it**: in a regular collection, taking a card's quantity down to 0 removes the line instead of leaving a 0× row behind. Set collections are unaffected — their qty-0 rows are the checklist and stay put.
