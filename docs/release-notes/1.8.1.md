# TCG Binder 1.8.1

Old printings of current cards are legal — as the rules say.

## Fixed

- **Reprint rule in deck validation**: an older printing (say, Ultra Ball from SVI) was flagged as not Standard-legal even though a current reprint makes the card legal to play. Legality is now shared across every printing of the same card, matched by canonical name so it works across languages: instantly when any card note in your binder proves it, and via a quick card-database lookup otherwise — the result is stamped on the card note (`legal-by-reprint`), so it's checked once and remembered.
